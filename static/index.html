Fit Notes is an AI shopping assistant for people who want clothing recommendations backed by real customer fit experiences. It explores patterns in clothing reviews to help you find products that better match your body, usual size, and preferences.

What makes Fit Notes different? Recommendations are grounded in a clothing dataset and fit information extracted from customer reviews—not just a language model's general knowledge. The available evidence varies by product, and reviewer height or size information may not always be available.

Your fit search

Fit Notes is a review-based clothing recommendation research project. Product prices and fit patterns come from the available dataset; availability, current prices, and inventory are not live-checked. Fit information reflects customer reviews and should be treated as guidance, not a guarantee of how an item will fit.
