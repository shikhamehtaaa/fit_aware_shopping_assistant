At 5'4" and 140 lbs, you have a very versatile frame. Being "between sizes" usually means your fit depends on the fabric (stretch vs. woven) and where you carry your weight (bust vs. hips).

Since you want something **flowy**, here are the three best styles for your proportions and tips on how to pick between the Medium and the Large.

### 1. The Tiered Midi Dress
This is the "gold standard" for flowy summer dresses. Because you are 5'4", a **midi** length (hitting mid-calf) often fits like a "maxi" would on a taller person, but without dragging on the floor.
*   **Why it works:** The tiers add volume without requiring a perfect fit at the hips.
*   **Sizing Tip:** If the dress has a **smocked (stretchy) bodice**, go with the **Medium**. The stretch will accommodate your chest, and the flowy bottom will provide plenty of room. If the fabric is a stiff cotton with a zipper, go with the **Large** for comfort.

### 2. The Adjustable Wrap Dress
Wrap dresses are ideal for people between sizes because you can cinch the waist exactly where you need it. 
*   **Why it works:** It creates a "V" neckline which elongates the torso, making you look taller. Look for a "high-low" hemline to show a little leg and prevent the fabric from overwhelming your 5'4" frame.
*   **Sizing Tip:** **Size up to the Large.** Wrap dresses can sometimes gap at the chest or leg if they are too tight. You can always tie the waist tighter, but you can’t add more fabric to the skirt.

### 3. The A-Line "Nap Dress" or Empire Waist
This style fits close to the bust and then floats away from the body starting just under the ribs.
*   **Why it works:** It highlights the narrowest part of your torso and hides any midsection concerns, creating a very airy, flowy feel.
*   **Sizing Tip:** **Go with the Medium.** These dresses are designed with a lot of "ease" (extra fabric) in the skirt. A Large might end up looking too "tented" or baggy on a 5'4" frame.

### Key Tips for Your Height & Weight:

*   **Fabric is King:** Look for **Rayon or Viscose**. These fabrics "drape" and move with you. **100% Cotton** or **Linen** can sometimes be stiff and "stand out," which can make you look wider if the dress is very oversized.
*   **The "Shoulder Rule":** When you are between sizes, check the shoulder seams. If the shoulders are too wide (common in a Large), the whole dress will look like it’s wearing you. If the dress has spaghetti straps, size doesn't matter as much; if it has sleeves, the Medium will likely look more "tailored."
*   **Length Check:** Aim for a length of **44–48 inches** from the shoulder. This is the sweet spot for 5'4" to get that long, flowy look without needing a tailor.

### Brand Recommendations for your build:

*   **Old Navy:** They offer "Petite" lengths in their flowy dresses. A **Medium-Petite** would likely be your perfect "dream fit."
*   **Abercrombie & Fitch:** Their "Emerson" or "Best Dressed Guest" lines are famous for having hidden elastic and adjustable straps—great for being between sizes. (Try the Large for a more relaxed look).

**The Verdict:** If the dress is **stretchy/smocked**, buy the **Medium**. If the dress is **stiff/woven (no stretch)**, buy the **Large**.
